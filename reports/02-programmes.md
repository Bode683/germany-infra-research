# Package 2 — Programme discovery

Date: 2026-10-24. Full inventory: `programmes.csv` (32 rows). Sources: S016–S023.

## Method

- Pulled all **1,592 English-taught master's** in the DAAD International Programmes database (S016) and filtered to CS / applied CS / IT / software / communications / security subjects: 149 programmes.
- Removed off-target fields (data science, ML, embedded hardware, HCI, bio/medical, business) and private universities (SRH, Fresenius, HDBW: tuition of several thousand euros per semester). Read the DAAD detail pages of 44 programmes.
- Added programmes missing from the DAAD list by checking official university sites for the large job markets: TU Berlin, TU Darmstadt, THI Ingolstadt.
- **German-taught programmes were not searched systematically.** They generally require German B2–C1 (THI's cloud master asks B2 — S021). A B1 candidate cannot use them. If you certify B2 by spring 2027, this changes; see finding 4.

**Limits:** the DAAD database covers "international" programmes only, so some English-taught programmes are surely missing. Curriculum depth was verified on official pages for TU Berlin, TU Darmstadt, Fulda and THI only. For the rest, infrastructure content is **UNKNOWN** until the module handbooks are read; that is moved to the Package 3 deep-dive. Several university sites were unreachable from this environment (Frankfurt UAS down for a security incident, Bingen blocked).

## Findings that change the plan

1. **Your English is native, but almost every programme still asks for a test certificate.** Nearly all list IELTS/TOEFL. Exemptions are "see website", decided programme by programme. Seven of the strongest options demand **C1 (IELTS 6.5–7)**: Darmstadt, Saarland, Bochum, Göttingen, TUHH, Bonn, Paderborn. **Recommendation: take IELTS Academic now, target 7.0+.** It costs about €200–250 and removes a failure point from every application. Relying on the EMI letter is the cheap option that can sink an application at the formal-check stage.

2. **Deadlines collide with the visa queue.** An admission letter dated August or September 2027 is close to useless when Yaoundé appointments take up to a year. Programmes with early decisions (Saarland 15 Nov → Feb, Bochum 15 Dec, RWTH/TUHH 1 Mar, Offenburg 31 Mar) are worth more than their rank suggests. Late-deadline programmes (TU Berlin 31 Aug, Kiel/Rhine-Waal 15 Jul) realistically mean **summer 2028**. Also ask the embassy whether an appointment can be used with an admission letter that arrives during the waiting period.

3. **Your grade is the gate for most HAWs.** Every HAW with a stated cut-off uses 2.5, your exact converted grade. One unfavourable rounding or weighting decision by uni-assist and the HAW route narrows sharply. Open-admission universities (TU Berlin, TU Darmstadt) don't select on grade but test content instead (theory credits, GRE). **Get a uni-assist VPD early.** It gives you the official converted grade before you spend money on applications.

4. **The best curriculum match is German-taught.** THI's Cloud Applications and Security Engineering (cloud-native development, CI/CD, orchestration, network security, €1,200/semester) is exactly the target profile, and it requires **German B2**. Certifying B2 by spring 2027 would reopen THI and many German-taught HAW programmes. It would also lift working-student job access in Package 3. That's the strongest argument for pushing German harder than English.

5. **Correction to Package 1:** Bavarian HAWs also charge non-EU tuition (THI €1,200, Deggendorf €500; S023). It's still under the €1,500 filter, so no candidate changes.

6. **Munich and Hamburg are effectively closed** at this grade and budget: TUM is too expensive and TUHH needs very good grades plus C1. Berlin is open only through TU Berlin's theory-credit gate. Rhine-Main is the most accessible large market: Darmstadt, Bingen, Fulda.

## Actions for the candidate

1. **Check your transcript against TU Berlin's requirement:** do you have ≥12 CP in automata/formal languages, computability, complexity or logic? Algorithms and discrete maths don't count. Fill in TU Darmstadt's Academic Eligibility Checklist (linked from its programme page). These two decide whether the two strongest market options are real.
2. **Book IELTS Academic** (target 7.0) as early as possible. Saarland's 15 Nov early deadline is only reachable if the EMI letter is accepted or a test result arrives in time; otherwise use its 15 May regular deadline.
3. **Request a uni-assist VPD** for one programme to get your official German grade.
4. Decide on the **GRE**: it opens Darmstadt (exam exemption) and Paderborn.
